Regulated Entities

5xprozone's entities are regulated by the Financial Services Regulatory Authority (FSRA) of the Abu Dhabi Global Market (ADGM) as follows:

  1. 5xprozone Exchange Limited is recognised as a Recognised Investment Exchange (Derivatives), with a stipulation to Operate a Multilateral Trading Facility;
  2. 5xprozone Clearing and Custody Limited is recognised as a Recognised Clearing House, with a stipulation to Provide Custody and operating a Central Securities Depository;
  3. 5xprozone Trading Limited is authorised to carry out the following Regulated Activities: (i) Dealing in Investments as Principal; (ii) Dealing in Investments as Agent; (iii) Arranging Deals in Investments; (iv) Managing Assets; (v) Providing Money Services; and (vi) Arranging Custody.
